## Characters

| Asset | Author | Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MakeHuman 1.x hand (hm08 base mesh, default skeleton and weights, male macro targets) | MakeHuman team (© Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier; targets © 2014 Manuel Bastioni) | [makehumancommunity/makehuman](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman) | CC0 | Gloved hands and finger weights of the enemy bodies in `Art/Characters/ValveBiped/Enemies` |

The four enemy bodies (Infantry, Charger, Shooter, Elite) were generated for the project with Hunyuan3D, then
refitted and skinned to the xdR ValveBiped skeleton.

The hero's mesh and textures (`Art/Characters/ValveBiped/Hero`) were generated for the project with Tripo, then
skinned to the xdR ValveBiped skeleton. Its grips were posed by eye after Valve's CS:GO GIGN third-person
model, which served only as a visual reference and is not shipped.

The CS:GO pack manifests list the Valve and Hidden Path original assets separately from the RAGNA SS pack credit. They explicitly name the AK and MP5 world models and the AK, MP5 and XM1014 viewmodel sources; they do not give a separate author or licence line for the project's renamed `w_xm1014` path. The xdR manifest lists xDShot's port work separately from Valve's original motion data. No additional author or licence is stated for the procedural sleeve textures.

The supply shed and watchtower shelter were generated for the project with Meshy and reworked, the
loaded bicycle with Hunyuan3D (decimated, normal map baked) and the crate stencils with an image
generator; the entrenching tool, the bunker, watchtower and cookfire timberwork, and the trellis,
hammock and camouflage net (on the flora's materials, with cloth and net textures from
Art/Source/gen_station_textures.py) are the project's own.
