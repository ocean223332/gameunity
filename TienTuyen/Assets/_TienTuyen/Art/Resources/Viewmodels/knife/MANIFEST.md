# First-person knife: CS:GO default T knife viewmodel
- Animations: GameBanana WiP 48384 "Source Weapons | GO Animations" (https://gamebanana.com/wips/48384), Google61, downloaded 2026-10-11, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 as stated; credit Google61 (rig, porting, compiling), Ghost1507512 (hand rig), CS PRO (models), Valve (original everything). Source: `finished/Knife/models/weapons/v_knife_t.mdl` (compiled from `v_knife_default_t/`), sequences idle1, draw, light_hit1, light_hit2, decoded with clip-source's own readers (srcmesh.py, srcanim.py).
- Arms: replaced on import by the hero's own forearms and hands, as for the guns; the FBX carries v_ak47's glove arms rebound to this skeleton only as their stand-in.
- Knife mesh: `knife_t_model.smd` from the same source, fitted rigidly onto its in-hand reference knife; it has the CS2 default T knife's UVs and wears that model's base colour (`v_knife.fbm/knife_t.png`; Sketchfab 5274207a087a455b86f68948facae4e5, alyax, CC BY 4.0; Valve model and textures).
- Takes: idle1, draw, slash1 (light_hit1), slash2 (light_hit2), 30 fps. `v_knife.takes.json` "done": the draw at frame 12 (knife up and settled); each slash where its blade crosses the middle of the view, which the game lands on its knife contact.
- `v_knife.view.json`: the vertical field of view the takes frame CS:GO's view at (53.6, CS:GO viewmodel_fov 68 at 4:3), checked against CS:GO footage.
- Built in output/clip-source/gb-source-weapons-go-anims/knife_vm (steps in its README.md); first-person arms are the hero's, from HeroArms_v_knife.json (ViewmodelArmsPostprocessor).
